Where it started

How we met

Our first meeting began at an unassuming Asian diner in Sunnyvale, followed by an adventure through the chai aisle at a nearby Indian supermarket, and finished with dessert at Molly Tea. We both knew something was special about each other right away.

Soon after

As time went on

We traveled to new destinations, accomplished new goals, and took on new challenges (like running a 10K and planting trees!). We've pushed each other to be the best version of ourselves physically and personally, but always remembering to stop to enjoy a cup of tea.

Recently

We got married!

On August 25th, 2026, we were officially joined in marriage at the lovely San Francisco City Hall! This, too, was followed by lunch and milk tea.
